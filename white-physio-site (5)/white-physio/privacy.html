White Physiotherapy Clinic ("White," "we," "us") provides home-visit physiotherapy, TeleRehab (live online physiotherapy sessions) and in-person care at our FlexRecovery Studio locations. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, our booking page and WhatsApp, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. What we collect

Depending on how you contact us or which form you use, we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, WhatsApp number, phone number, email address.
  • Location: your state and area, so we can quote a home-visit price and assign a nearby physiotherapist.
  • Health information: the condition troubling you (for example, back pain, sciatica, a nerve injury), and anything else you choose to share about your symptoms, on our booking page or on WhatsApp, so a physiotherapist can plan safe and appropriate care.
  • Payment-related information: proof of payment you send us (for example, a bank transfer receipt), for TeleRehab and home-visit bookings. We do not collect or store your card or bank account details — payment itself happens by bank transfer, and any card or transfer details you enter are handled by your own bank or payment app, not by us.
  • Website usage: standard technical information most websites collect automatically (such as your browser type and pages visited), used only to keep the site working well and secure.

2. How we collect it

We collect information when you fill in a form on this website, message us on WhatsApp, use our booking page, or speak with a member of our team by phone. Our booking page is operated by a third-party booking platform (an EHR system) — when you book a TeleRehab or home-visit session there, that platform's own privacy terms also apply, alongside this one.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and book your home visit, TeleRehab session or studio appointment
  • To quote an accurate price for your location
  • To assign the right physiotherapist and plan your care safely
  • To confirm and follow up on payment for a booking
  • To send appointment reminders and follow-up messages on WhatsApp
  • To improve our services and this website

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with:

  • The physiotherapist assigned to your case, so they can prepare for and deliver your treatment
  • Our booking platform (EHR), to schedule and manage your appointment

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to ask us what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it if it's wrong, and to ask us to delete it, subject to any legal or medical record-keeping requirements we must follow. To make a request, message us on WhatsApp or email whitephysiotherapy@gmail.com.

8. Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It may use basic, temporary browser storage only to remember your progress if you're partway through the home-visit price checker, which is never shared with anyone and clears when you close your browser.

9. Children

Our services are for adults booking care for themselves or a family member. If you are booking care for a child, this policy applies to the information you provide on their behalf.
