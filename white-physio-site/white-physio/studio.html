Common questions

Your questions, answered

How do I book a visit to the studio?

Tap Contact Ota Studio or Contact Osogbo Studio and send us a message on WhatsApp. Tell us where it hurts and how long you have had it. Our team will reply and agree a day and time with you.

Where are your studios?

We have studios in Ota (Ogun State) and Osogbo (Osun State). When you message us, we will send you the full address and tell you how to find us.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothes that you can move in. If you have a scan, an X-ray or a doctor's report, bring it. If you have none, that is fine.

How much will it cost?

The price depends on the treatment you need. We will tell you the price before you start. Message the studio nearest to you and we will explain.

Do I need a doctor's referral first?

Usually not. You can contact us directly. If your condition needs a doctor's opinion, your physiotherapist will tell you.

What if I cannot travel to the studio?

We can come to your home instead. Home visits are available nationwide. You can also start TeleRehab, our live online sessions, from home. Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose.
