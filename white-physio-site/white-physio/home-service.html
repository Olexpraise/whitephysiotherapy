The honest truth

Why drugs, massage and quick fixes keep failing you

Most people come to us after trying several of these. Here is what each one does, and what it leaves behind.

  • Pain medication

    Calms the pain for a few hours. The cause of the pain stays where it was.

  • Massage

    Loosens tight muscles for a while. It does not fix a pinched nerve or a poor posture.

  • Herbal remedies

    May ease the pain a little. They do not build back weak muscles or teach your body to move well again.

  • Random exercises from the internet

    Without a check-up first, you can strain a joint that is already weak and make the pain worse.

  • Now and then clinic visits

    One or two visits a month is rarely enough. Getting better needs regular, steady treatment.

None of these are useless. Pain medicine, for one, has its place. But on their own, they rarely fix what is causing your pain.

Understanding your options

Physiotherapy and pain medication

They do different jobs. For many conditions, both have a place.

Pain medication

Medicine can help with symptoms like pain and swelling. But it may not fix:

  • Movement problems
  • Weak muscles
  • Stiff joints
  • Loss of strength
  • Poor posture and movement habits
  • Trouble with daily activities

Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy works on how you move and how your body works, through:

  • Exercises made for your condition
  • Mobility training
  • Strengthening
  • Hands-on treatment where it is right for you
  • Help to get back to daily life
  • Advice and education
  • A home exercise plan

Medicine can be useful for easing symptoms. Physiotherapy works on your movement and strength. Depending on your condition, you may benefit from both.

Never stop or change medicine your doctor gave you without speaking to them first.

Questions patients ask

Your questions, answered

How do I book a home visit?

Tap Book My Home Visit, choose your state and area and what is troubling you, and see your price straight away. Then you continue on our booking page to pick your visit. If you prefer, you can message us on WhatsApp instead.

Which areas do you visit?

We offer home visits nationwide. Choose your state and area when you book and you will see your price. If your area is not listed, message us on WhatsApp and we will confirm if a physiotherapist covers you.

Who exactly comes to my house?

One of our own physiotherapists. You will know who is coming before they arrive, and the same person comes back for each visit.

How much will it cost?

The price depends on where you live and the treatment you need. We will tell you the price for your area before you book.

How soon can treatment start?

Many people can start within a few days of booking. It depends on your area and the physiotherapist's diary. We will agree a date with you on WhatsApp.

What should I bring or prepare for my first visit?

Wear loose, comfortable clothes you can move in, and keep a clear space at home where your physiotherapist can treat you, ideally with a bed or firm sofa and a chair. Have any scans (X-ray or MRI), doctor's notes and a list of your medicines ready to show. A family member is welcome to sit in. Your physiotherapist brings the treatment equipment.

How detailed is the first assessment?

It is thorough but gentle. Your physiotherapist asks about your pain, your health history and how the problem affects your daily life. They check your posture, movement, strength and the painful area, then explain what they find in simple words before any treatment starts. You can ask questions or ask them to stop at any time.

When should I think about surgery instead?

Some conditions do need surgery, for example a serious fracture, severe nerve pressure or a badly damaged joint, and that is a decision for your doctor. For many back, joint and muscle problems, physiotherapy is often tried first. If your physiotherapist sees signs that you need a doctor or surgeon, they will tell you honestly and refer you. If your doctor has already recommended surgery, please follow that advice.

Is this cheaper than going to a hospital?

For many conditions, physiotherapy costs much less than surgery. Your physiotherapist will also tell you honestly if hospital care is what you need.

Do I need a doctor's referral first?

Usually not. You can contact us directly. If your condition needs a doctor's opinion, your physiotherapist will tell you.

Should I stop my pain medication?

No. Never stop or change medicine your doctor gave you without speaking to them first. Physiotherapy can work alongside your medicine.

What if there is no physiotherapist in my area yet?

We will tell you honestly. You may be able to visit our FlexRecovery Studio, or start TeleRehab, our live online sessions, at home. Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose.

What if the treatment is not working for me?

Your physiotherapist checks your progress at every visit and changes your plan as you improve. If something worries you, tell us on WhatsApp at any time.
