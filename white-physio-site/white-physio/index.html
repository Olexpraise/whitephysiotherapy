Common questions

Your Questions, Answered

How does the 30-day programme work?

You register, then get daily exercise videos on WhatsApp for 30 days. Each session takes about 15 to 20 minutes. Your physiotherapist checks on you and answers your questions.

Can I get better at home without going to a clinic?

For many common pains, yes. This includes sciatica, knee pain and neck pain. The exercises are gentle and guided step by step. If your case needs a doctor or a clinic, we will tell you.

Will a physiotherapist visit me in my city?

We visit many areas. Send us your location on WhatsApp and we will tell you if we can reach you.

What do I need for the home programme?

A phone with WhatsApp is the main thing. Most exercises need no special equipment. A chair and a towel or mat are usually enough.

Is it safe for people over 60?

Yes, the programme is made for older adults too. It starts easy and builds slowly. A family member can follow along and help you. If you have a serious health condition, check with your doctor first.

How can I be sure White Physiotherapy is real?

You can watch real patient stories on this page. We only accept payment into our official bank account, and you can call or WhatsApp us before you pay. We will never ask you to pay a third person or use a payment link.
