About Corporate Wellness

Physiotherapy that comes to your workplace

Corporate Wellness is a programme where our licensed physiotherapists come to your office and look after the people who keep your business running. We look at how your team sits, moves and works, treat the pain that desk work causes, and teach simple habits that stop it coming back.

Pain that long hours at a desk can lead to

  • 🧍
    Lower back pain and sciaticaSitting for hours strains the lower back and can irritate the nerves that run down the leg.
  • 💻
    Neck pain and cervical spondylosisLooking down at screens and phones puts steady strain on the neck, which can cause stiffness, headaches and tingling in the arms.
  • 🪑
    Buttock and hip pain (piriformis syndrome)Long sitting can tighten the muscle that presses on the sciatic nerve.
  • ⌨️
    Shoulder, wrist and hand painTyping and mouse work can overload the shoulders, wrists and hands.
How it helps

How Corporate Wellness prevents these problems

Most desk-related pain builds up slowly. We step in early, so small aches do not turn into long-term conditions.

🔍

Early screening

We check posture, spine and joint movement, so we catch problems before they become chronic.

🖥️

Ergonomic set-up

We advise on chair, desk and screen height, so each person sits in a way that spares the back and neck.

🤲

Hands-on treatment

Non-invasive physiotherapy eases existing pain and stiffness, so staff stay at work and keep working well.

🧘

Simple movement habits

Short stretches and strengthening exercises that fit into a working day, so the benefits last.
