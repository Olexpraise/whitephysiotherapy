The honest truth

Why drugs, massage and quick fixes have not given you lasting relief

You have probably already spent a lot on:

  • Pain relief drugs. They reduce the feeling of pain for a few hours. When they wear off, the pain is back. Nothing inside your neck has changed.

  • Massage therapy. It relaxes the muscles on the surface for a while. It cannot take pressure off the nerves in your neck, so the numbness and shooting pain return.

  • “It is just ageing, so manage it.” Being told to simply live with the pain is not good enough. Neck problems like this can often be helped with the right physiotherapy, whatever your age.

  • Surgery. Some people are told they need surgery, and some have even had it, yet the pain came back. Surgery can correct the structure, but without good rehabilitation the muscles can stay weak and the nerve can stay easy to irritate.

  • Random neck exercises from YouTube or WhatsApp. Risky. The wrong neck exercise can press on the nerves even more and make things worse.

  • Now and then clinic visits. A short visit once a week is not enough to change the habits that strain your neck every day.

The real causes of your pain

Cervical spondylosis and shoulder pain do not go away with drugs or massage, because the real causes are in your neck, your nerves and your muscles:

  • Nerve compression in the neck. The bones or discs in your neck are pressing on the nerves that travel down your arm. That causes the shooting pain, numbness and tingling in your fingers.

  • Worn discs. The cushioning discs between your neck bones have worn down or bulged, leaving less room for the nerve to move freely.

  • Weak neck and shoulder muscles. When the muscles that support your neck are weak, more pressure lands on the joints and nerves.

  • Tight muscles. Tight neck, shoulder and upper back muscles pull your neck out of good alignment every day.

  • Daily posture habits. How you hold your phone, sit at your desk or sleep may be adding pressure to your neck without you noticing.

The only way to lasting relief is to deal with these root causes through the right, step-by-step physiotherapy exercises, done consistently and with guidance. That is what this programme is built to do.

Your 30-day recovery journey, phase by phase

Three phases, each building on the last. Just 15 to 20 minutes of guided exercises each morning.

    Phase 1: Relieve pain and ease pressure on the nerve

    Goal: Begin easing pressure on the nerve in your neck and give your neck and shoulder relief.

    • Very gentle neck decompression exercises, safe even when your pain is severe
    • Targeted stretches to loosen tight neck and upper back muscles, without harm
    • The right way to sleep, sit and hold your phone, from Day 1
    • Cold and heat therapy guidance for pain relief at home
    • Start the anti-inflammatory food plan
  2. 2 Days 11 to 20

    Phase 2: Strengthen the neck, shoulder and upper back

    Goal: Rebuild the muscles that support your neck and shoulder joint.

    • Step-by-step neck strengthening to support the joints in your neck
    • Shoulder stabilisation training, to rebuild the muscles that guide your arm movement
    • Upper back strengthening to correct the forward head posture that may be making things worse
    • Nerve mobilisation techniques to help the nerves move more freely down your arm
    • Make your own anti-inflammatory herbal tea and pain-relief balm from local Nigerian ingredients
  3. 3 Days 21 to 30

    Phase 3: Restore movement and keep it

    Goal: Return to your normal daily activities and help the pain stay away.

    • Work towards turning your neck fully, left and right, looking up and down, with less pain
    • Return to daily activities: driving, cooking, lifting and working at a desk
    • Exercises for finger feeling and grip strength, if you have numbness or weakness
    • Simple weight management guidance, because extra weight adds pressure to your neck
    • Maintenance exercises to help keep your neck healthy for the long term

Honest answers

Your questions, answered honestly

Will these exercises actually work for my cervical spondylosis?

Many people with neck and shoulder pain feel better when the tight muscles are eased, the supporting muscles are strengthened and the habits that strain the neck are changed. That is what the programme guides you through. Results differ from person to person, so we cannot promise the same result for everyone. That is why you watch the free sample first.

I am afraid to move my neck. What if the exercises make things worse?

It is natural to be careful with your neck. Phase 1 (Days 1 to 10) uses very gentle movements, made to be safe even when the pain is strong. Go slowly and stay within what feels comfortable. If an exercise clearly makes the pain, numbness or tingling worse, stop and message us on WhatsApp. If you notice new weakness in your arms or legs, trouble walking or balancing, or trouble controlling your bladder or bowels, stop and see a doctor urgently.

My doctor said I need cervical surgery. Should I still try this first?

That is a decision to make with your doctor, and this programme does not replace medical advice. If your doctor has said you need surgery, ask them whether it is safe to try guided exercises first, and for how long. If you have had cervical surgery in the last 6 weeks, please do not book yet. Message us on WhatsApp if you are not sure.

I have tried physiotherapy before and it did not help. Why will this be different?

A short visit once a week often does not change the daily habits that strain your neck. This programme gives you guided exercises every morning, weekly check-ins on WhatsApp and advice on how you sleep, sit and hold your phone. It may still not suit everyone, which is why the sample video comes first.

I am elderly. Can I still do these exercises safely?

The exercises start gentle and slow, and you go at your own pace. A family member can join the live session with you if that helps. If you have a heart condition, weak bones, dizziness or any other health problem, check with your doctor before you start.

I am busy. Can I really find 15 to 20 minutes every morning?

Yes. Sessions are usually 15 to 20 minutes, held live online so you can join from your phone, tablet or computer. We work with you to find times that fit your day.
