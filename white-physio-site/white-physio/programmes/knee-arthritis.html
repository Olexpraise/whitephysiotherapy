The honest truth

Why drugs, massage and quick fixes have not given you lasting relief

You have probably already spent a lot on:

  • Pain relief drugs. They reduce the feeling of pain for a few hours. When they wear off, the pain is back. The wear and swelling inside your knee have not changed.

  • Steroid injections. They can ease the pain for a short time. But injections do not rebuild cartilage or strengthen the muscles around your knee, so the pain returns when they wear off.

  • Massage therapy. It relaxes the muscles for a while. It does not deal with the worn cartilage, joint swelling or weak muscles behind your knee pain, so the relief does not last.

  • “It is old age, so manage it.” Being told to simply live with the pain is not good enough. Knee arthritis can often be helped with the right physiotherapy. Getting older does not mean you must live in pain forever.

  • Surgery advice. You may have been told you need knee replacement surgery, and some people have had it, yet the pain returned. Surgery can correct the structure, but without good rehabilitation the muscles can stay weak and the joint can stay vulnerable.

  • Random exercises from YouTube or WhatsApp. Risky for knee arthritis. The wrong exercise can put extra strain on a worn joint and make things worse.

The real causes of your knee pain

Knee arthritis and joint pain do not go away with drugs or massage, because the real causes are inside and around your knee joint:

  • Worn cartilage. The cushioning cartilage in your knee has worn thin or broken down. When the bones rub against each other, you get that sharp pain and the grinding sound you hear.

  • Weak thigh and leg muscles. Your thigh and hamstring muscles are meant to absorb the load on your knee. When they are weak, that pressure goes straight onto the damaged joint.

  • Long-lasting joint swelling. A constantly inflamed knee brings the swelling, heat and stiffness that lock you in place, especially in the morning.

  • Poor movement patterns. The way you walk, climb stairs and stand up from a chair may be adding extra pressure to your knee every single day without you noticing.

  • Extra body weight. Extra weight adds a lot of pressure to your knee joint with every step. Managing your weight is an important part of knee recovery.

The only way to lasting relief is to deal with these root causes through the right, step-by-step knee exercises, done consistently and with guidance. That is what this programme is built to do.

Honest answers

Your questions, answered honestly

Will these exercises actually work for my knee arthritis?

Many people with knee arthritis feel better when the muscles around the knee are strengthened, the joint is moved gently and daily movement habits are corrected. That is what the programme guides you through. Exercise does not rebuild worn cartilage, but stronger muscles can take load off the joint and ease pain. Results differ from person to person, so we cannot promise the same result for everyone. That is why you watch the free sample first.

I am afraid the exercises will make my knee pain worse.

That is a common worry. Phase 1 (Days 1 to 10) uses very gentle movements, made to be safe even when the knee is painful or swollen. Go slowly and stay within what feels bearable. If an exercise makes the pain sharply worse, or your knee gives way, stop and message us on WhatsApp. If your knee suddenly becomes very swollen, hot and red, or you have a fever, or you cannot put any weight on the leg, see a doctor urgently.

My doctor said I need knee replacement surgery. Should I try this first?

That is a decision to make with your doctor, and this programme does not replace medical advice. Some people with knee arthritis find that stronger muscles and better movement help them cope better, but it depends on your knee. Ask your doctor whether it is safe to try guided exercises first, and for how long. If you have had knee surgery in the last 6 weeks, please do not register yet.

I have tried physiotherapy before and it did not help. Why will this be different?

A short visit once or twice a week often does not change the daily movement habits that put pressure on your knee. This programme gives you guided exercises every morning, weekly check-ins on WhatsApp and advice on how you walk, sit and stand. It may still not suit everyone, which is why the sample video comes first.

I am elderly. Can I still safely do these exercises?

The exercises start gentle and slow, and you go at your own pace. A family member can help you follow the videos, and you may want a sturdy chair or a wall nearby for balance. If you have a heart condition, weak bones, dizziness or any other health problem, check with your doctor before you start.

I am busy. Can I really do 15 to 20 minutes every morning?

Yes. Each session is 15 to 20 minutes, done at home by following a video on your phone or TV. You can do it before your day gets busy.
